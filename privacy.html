Privacy Policy - African Soul Voices

Last updated: October 2026

African Soul Voices is a personal publishing tool, not offered to the public. It is used by its owner to upload their own original content to their own TikTok account. It has no other users.

What the tool accesses

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool an authorization token. The tool uses this token only to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and manually confirmed. It does not read or collect information about any other TikTok user.

Where data is kept

The authorization token and the videos stay on the owner's own computer, in encrypted local storage. They are not sent to this website or to any server of the tool. They are not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties, other than the upload itself to TikTok.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and it collects no personal data. GitHub, as host, may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy statement.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the app permissions in their TikTok account settings, and can delete the locally stored token.

The tool is provided as is, without warranty.
